## Question

Is this map planning or building — does it end at a reviewed spec, or at a
running harvester?

## Resolution

**A working harvester for all three CLIs.** The map carries execution, overriding
Wayfinder's plan-don't-do default via the map's Notes. Decision tickets still
resolve as decisions; implementation tickets graduate from the fog as their
decisions close.

Rejected: a spec-and-ADR handoff (Wayfinder's default, and the shape suggested by
the loose "maybe a custom encoding?" framing of the original ask); and a
decision-only map that locks just the substrate and defers everything else.
