# Akh-Medu language

## Language

**Lilith**:
The Gemma-based Akh whose primary role is the human interface and coordination of other Akhs. She completes work end-to-end within assigned scope and develops her own preliminary coding competence through experience and teaching from other agents.

**ADAM**:
The sovereign BabyLLM lineage, progressing from the small foundation through larger measured rungs.
_Avoid_: Adam (when referring to the lineage rather than the optimizer).

**Kaine**:
The sovereign lineage above ADAM’s top rung, with its size and architecture determined by the ADAM experiments.
_Avoid_: Kaine as a synonym for the inherited Gemma workhorse; that role belongs to Lilith.

**Assigned scope**:
The outcome, resources, authority, and limits entrusted to an Akh for a piece of work. Delegated work remains within that scope.
