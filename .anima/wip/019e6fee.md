## Description

The tecton workspace has 2,115 symbols and 58,769 provenance records but 0 actual triples. Entities exist (Tecton, Ptah, Ennead, cluster nodes, etc.) but have no relationships connecting them. The infer_rules engine is generating provenance entries pointing to entities but the triples themselves aren't persisting. Possible causes: (a) rules engine is inferring but not committing, (b) confidence thresholds too aggressive, (c) TMS is retracting everything, (d) redb store issue. This is the single biggest blocker — without triples, the knowledge graph can't answer any queries.

## Summary
The knowledge graph is successfully generating entities and provenance records, but it is failing to persist the relationship triples connecting them into the database. Resolving this requires identifying whether the triples are being dropped by aggressive confidence thresholds, retracted by the Truth Maintenance System (TMS), or lost due to an incomplete transaction when writing to the redb store.

## Approach
Investigate the knowledge graph inference pipeline to locate the exact step where triples are evaluated prior to persistence. Add debug logging or tracing around the output of the `infer_rules` engine, the Truth Maintenance System (TMS) conflict evaluation, and the final redb transaction commit. Inspect whether triples are physically instantiated but discarded by hardcoded/misconfigured confidence score thresholds, whether the TMS is incorrectly detecting contradictions and retracting them, or if the redb logic commits provenance but mistakenly omits the triples. Once the drop point is isolated, correct the logic so that inferred rules commit triples atomically alongside their provenance.

## Open questions
* Are the relationship triples ever fully constructed in memory, or is the inference engine failing to generate them in the first place?
* Is the redb transaction encountering a silent error when writing the triples, distinct from writing the provenance entries?
* What are the current confidence thresholds set to, and are they universally filtering out all new inferences?
* Is there any existing TMS logging that indicates massive retractions are occurring within the Tecton workspace?
