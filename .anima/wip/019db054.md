### What this phase is

Builds formal reasoning about what different agents know, believe, and don't know. Gives akh-medu the ability to model other agents' mental states (theory of mind) and use this to plan communicative actions strategically. Foundational for any multi-agent interaction.

### Key deliverables

- `EpistemicStateManager`: formal representation of what agents know/believe about propositions via accessibility relations
- Dynamic epistemic logic operations: public announcements, private messages, observations, questions, retractions with full update semantics
- `ToMEngine`: recursive belief modeling (Level 0–3) that predicts other agents' behavior and detects information asymmetries
- E-graph rewrite rules for epistemic inference (K-axiom, introspection, public announcement)
- ToM-based communication strategy: what to share, withhold, ask given goals and opponent models

### Dependencies

Phase 17 (evidence intervals), Phase 18 (source reliability), Phase 12d (interlocutor microtheories).

### Why this priority

Required for higher-level social intelligence (Phase 21) and communicative competence (Phase 34) — many things downstream depend on this.
